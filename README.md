# THE WALKING DEAD
**Genre:** **Horror** | **Seasons:** 11

## Top Characters
1. Rick Grimes - A former sheriff's deputy who wakes from a coma into the apocalypse and becomes the de facto leader of the primary survivor group.
2. Daryl Dixon - A rugged, loyal tracker and crossbow expert who evolves into one of the core pillars and leaders of the group.
3. Carol Peletier - A formerly timid housewife who transforms into a brilliant, ruthless, and highly strategic survivor.
4. Michonne - A stoic, katana-wielding warrior who joins Rick's group and becomes a central leader.
5. Maggie Greene / Rhee - The resilient daughter of Hershel who grows from a farm survivor into a hardened community leader.
6. Glenn Rhee- A quick-witted, compassionate former pizza delivery driver and key moral compass for the group.
